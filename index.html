About Me

Erin Koga is a student at San Jose State University studying Digital Media Art. Koga has lived in the Bay Area her entire life, and has become quite familiar with the East and South Bay area. An aspiring artist, she hopes to gain skills needed to work on projects for games or animations. All work in this portfolio was made during the Fall 2025 Semester for Art 74 and 75, as well as personal digital illustrations.
