About Me

I was born and raised in the bay area! I have an associates in art and in humanities that I obtained from Ohlone Community College, and am currently pursuing a bachelors in Digital Media Art at San Jose State University. My work at the moment mainly consists of design and illustrations. I am currently working on expanding it into more digital media work such as video and photo editing.
