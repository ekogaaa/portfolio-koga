Single Channel Video 1

Our first video project was a freestyle video that centered around one topic. The topic I chose for this video was the concept of love, and tackling the negatives and positive of it.

Single Channel Video 2

Our second video project had to have some part of ourself in it. I decided to keep the trend going with the focus of "love", and used flowers to depict an old game where you would pluck petals off to decide on fate.

Single Channel Video 3

For our third project, we were to edit together a sound composition and then create a video to go with the sound. This project I decided to change gears, and go with something a bit more abstract. The rain and water sound was made first, using audio I recorded to merge it together to be a smooth moving sound that goes from strong to weak. I then applied this video to the concept of coin pushers, using the sound comparison to hopefully draw a connection between the two while the video showed this.

Abstract Video

The fourth project was to create something abstract. Thinking on this project, I wanted to create an abstract absurdist piece. Drawing things inspired by the artist Bruno Pontiroli and his work, I animated a video with an absurd title.

Double Channel Video

Our final project was to create a multi channel video (two, three, or four channels). I used two channels to keep it simple, but also to keep the topic of my video simple. I wanted to tackle how someone can feel anxiety through walking alone through a crowded or empty area. To show this I had taken two videos, one of me walking through a mall area in the day, and one of me walking alone on a different campus at night.
