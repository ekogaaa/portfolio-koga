Digital Animation

I wanted to replicate the process of playing a visual novel in Adobe After Effects. I created all of the assets seen in the game such as the "menu" screen, the text box, and the character sprites, which were done in Procreate. Backgrounds are stock images that I took into Photoshop to edit and change into what I deemed fit for the environment. Editing the entire thing together was something that was tedious, but I wanted the video to reflect my vision of a game I would want to create or play in the future.

Abstract Video

I wanted to create an abstract absurdist piece. Drawing things inspired by the artist Bruno Pontiroli and his work, I drew out absurd shapes of fish. I wanted it to feel as odd as possible. Editing the video together, I experimented with the audio as well, seeing the limits and boundaries I could push just through After Effect. I had the most fun editing this video because the more I had completed, the weirder it felt, and that was exactly what I wanted. I wanted it to be nonsensical, to be confusing and simply weird.

Single Channel Video 1

This single channel video was created to be centered around love. I wanted it to feel slow, to drag on for a bit. I wanted the piece to feel personal even if it was shot with a more “professional” tone. This video had reflected what I personally felt like love was toward some people, and I wanted that to reflect in the recording.

Single Channel Video 2

For this video, I decided to keep the trend going with the focus of "love", and used flowers to depict an old French game where you would pluck petals off to decide on fate. It was something I used to do as a child since I saw it in movies and shows a lot, despite not really having anybody or thing to have this apply to. It was a cute callback to a childhood moment, doing it as a grown adult.

Single Channel Video 3

The rain and water sound was made first, using audio I recorded to merge it together to be a smooth moving sound that goes from strong to weak. Editing the audio was half of the challenge, but as I was listening to it I kept thinking about how it felt similar to the rise and fall of chance games. Like you get a good run going, where things go in your favor, and then it slowly or gradually trickles down. The recorded video tries to reflect this mindset, with the visual effects added to imitate the “blurring” of time, especially in places or doing things that are “addictive” or try to keep you there with the promise of a reward.

Double Channel Video

I wanted to tackle how someone can feel anxiety through walking alone through a crowded or empty area. It was something that had come to mind when I personally experienced these things, one during the day and one at night. It puts into perspective how harrowing and truly scary these things can be, especially for women. Even if a place is crowded, stuff can happen in broad daylight. I wanted the video to reflect that, even in completely opposite situations, the feeling can stay the same.
